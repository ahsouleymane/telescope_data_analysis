# Sub-graph statistics per language

Statistics of the sub-graph induced by the channels of each language with at least 100 channels (edges with both ends in that language).

- `density`, `reciprocity`, `clustering` (global transitivity, undirected).
- `mean_degree` / `median_degree` / `max_degree`: undirected degree inside the language.
- `top1pct_degree_share`: share of degree held by the 1% best connected channels (hub concentration).
- `giant_component_share`: share of channels in the largest weakly connected component.
- `out_to_other_language` / `in_from_other_language`: edges linking the language to a channel of another known language.

Generated by `code/language_analysis.py`.
